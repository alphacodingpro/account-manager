# Product roadmap

## Milestone 1 — Foundation

- [x] Authentication and profile
- [x] Company onboarding and multi-company switcher
- [x] Branch creation and live branch list
- [x] Chart of Accounts and balanced journal engine foundation
- [x] Responsive white/blue application shell
- [ ] Company invitations and permission editor

## Milestone 2 — Sales and purchases

- Customers, suppliers, quotations and orders
- GST invoices, receipts, bills, payments, credit/debit notes
- Receivable/payable aging and PDF documents

## Milestone 3 — Inventory

- Products, services, units, warehouses and branch stock
- Average-cost valuation, transfers, adjustments and reorder alerts

## Milestone 4 — Reporting

- General Ledger, Trial Balance, Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow
- Branch comparison, dashboard drill-down and CSV/Excel export

## Milestone 5 — Payroll

- Attendance, leave, salary, allowances, deductions and loans
- Configurable EOBI, SESSI and income-tax rules with effective dates
- Payslips and automatic accounting entries
